How sustainable sharing works.

Do

  • Create original jokes, art and commentary.
  • Link to the official site or exact CA.
  • Disclose any payment, gift or project relationship in the post itself.
  • Correct outdated figures and distinguish snapshots from live data.
  • Join relevant conversations with something useful to add.

Do not

  • Promise returns, targets or a graduation date.
  • Buy followers, reactions, comments or coordinated fake accounts.
  • Spam unrelated chats, hashtags, replies or direct messages.
  • Imply a partnership, listing or endorsement that is not documented.
  • Hide creator concentration or the token's current curve status.

Collaboration standard

Cross-promotion should have a real audience fit, original co-created content and clear disclosure of any material connection. No paid placement is official until its terms, budget source and disclosure language are agreed in writing.

Safety line: admins never DM first, never ask for seed phrases and never request an unannounced transfer. BAGSY is a speculative meme token, not financial advice.
