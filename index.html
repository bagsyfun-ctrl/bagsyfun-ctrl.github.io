FAQ

What does BAGSY do?

BAGSY is an open-source cartoon AI mascot for a Solana meme community. The agent is designed to read a designated public wallet, publish factual summaries and never trade its own token. The token itself has no revenue or promise of value and remains on the pump.fun bonding curve.

Is Bagsy an investment?

No. $BAGSY is a meme token — a joke with a mascot and a community. It has no product, no revenue and no promise of value. It can and often does go to zero. Only play with money you can afford to lose completely.

Does Bagsy give trading advice?

No. Bagsy's posts are entertainment. The open-source agent can read a designated public wallet after configuration; it does not manage anyone else's money. If you take financial advice from a cartoon robot, that is between you and the robot.

Is there a community treasury?

Not yet. Bonding-curve creator fees route to the creator/fee-recipient configured by the protocol. A separate community treasury should not be assumed unless its address, policy and reports are published.

Who is behind it?

The project is currently pseudonymous. The creator wallet is public on-chain. Never rely on a claimed identity, private message or unofficial contract address.
